Predict Purchase Behaviour
with Machine Learning

Enter a user's age and estimated salary to predict whether they are likely to purchase a product — powered by an ensemble of 3 trained models.

Try the Predictor →

🔍 Problem

  • Businesses struggle to identify which users will purchase after seeing a social network ad
  • Targeting the wrong audience wastes advertising budget and reduces conversion rates
  • A data-driven approach is needed to predict purchase behaviour from basic demographics

💡 Solution

  • Built an ML pipeline on a synthetic dataset of 2000+ users with real-world data issues
  • Three models trained and tuned — Logistic Regression, KNN, and SVM
  • Final prediction made by majority vote ensemble — 2 out of 3 models must agree

✅ Outcome

  • Enables businesses to precisely target potential customers for their products
  • Reduces advertising costs by focusing resources on high-probability users
  • Accurate behaviour forecasting improves conversion rates and revenue generation
