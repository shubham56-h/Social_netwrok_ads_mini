About This Project

ML Mini Project — Social Network Ads Purchase Prediction

Project Overview

Smart predictor is a platform that predicts whether a social network user will purchase a product based on their age and estimated salary. A synthetic dataset of 2000+ rows with real-world issues (missing values, outliers, negative entries, duplicates) cleaned through a structured preprocessing pipeline. Three models — Logistic Regression, KNN, and SVM — were trained and tuned individually, then combined into a majority-vote ensemble for the final prediction.
